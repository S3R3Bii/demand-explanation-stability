# When the reported demand driver changes, should an operations team trust it?

Study48, declared28October2026 before any fitting. This is an independent research example, not a new explanation algorithm, a causal business claim or a forecasting deployment. A negative or mixed result meets the study objective.

## Decision, source and availability

An analyst may present a model's highest-ranked weather driver as an actionable explanation. Measure whether that ranking survives modest changes in the historical training weeks, whether grouping correlated temperature measurements helps, and what predictive accuracy is lost or gained by simpler model families. Do not interpret model reliance as the effect of changing weather or business policy.

## Model families, complete search and baselines

Shared calendar map: hour sin/cos harmonics1 and2 with24-hour period, month sin/cos with12-month period, weekday one-hot codes0–6, weather one-hot codes1–4, workingday and holiday. Weather continuous descriptors are temp,atemp,hum,windspeed. One feature group's columns are always permuted together. Groups are hour,weekday,month,workingday,holiday,weather,temp,atemp,humidity,windspeed.

Linear ridge uses those calendar columns plus the four supplied continuous descriptors. Additive ridge replaces each continuous descriptor with cubic spline columns:5quantile knots fitted on the corresponding training rows, degree3, include_bias=False, extrapolation='linear'. Calendar terms remain additive; no interactions. These curves can be inspected, but human comprehensibility is not established by a user study. Fit population means/SDs of the design matrix inside each training sample, constant-column scale1. Intercept unpenalized. Lambda grid={0.0001,0.001,0.01,0.1,1}; solve mean squared residual+lambda*sum(feature_coefficients²), implemented by Ridge(alpha=n*lambda,solver='svd'). Retain design-column maps, knots, scalers and coefficients. Independently verify the corresponding normal-equation residual, fitted values and convex objective; no iterative or globally optimal generalization claim.

Small CART: DecisionTreeRegressor with max_depth={2,4,6},min_samples_leaf={20,60},criterion='squared_error',random_state=20261028. Six settings. Boosting: HistGradientBoostingRegressor with max_leaf_nodes={7,15},learning_rate={.05,.1},l2_regularization={0,1},max_iter=120,min_samples_leaf=30,max_bins=64,early_stopping=False,random_state=20261028,loss='squared_error'. Two simple baselines: training overall mean; training mean by hour×workingday, falling back to overall mean for unseen keys. These are fixed references, not selected by the test. ## Historical-week perturbations and fixed explanations

For each selected family, refit12versions by resampling complete2011Monday-start calendar weeks with replacement. Every row of a sampled week retains the same multiplicity; partial edge weeks are kept as such. Seed20261028; retain ordered week IDs, all draw indices, row multiplicities and per-fit settings. Hyperparameters are frozen, not retuned in each resample. The full2011fit is a separate reference, so four families×13fits=52fitted variants. Fit preprocessing afresh in every resampled training set. These perturbations describe conditional training-history sensitivity; they are not posterior model probabilities, independent deployment trials or an ordinary confidence interval.

Freeze all52models before any2012prediction or explanation. Score all2012rows for predictive MSE(count²),RMSE(count),MAE(count),mean error(count) and normalized MSE divided by2011training target variance. Report negative count predictions before clipping, by family/variant. Compare error with both baselines and preserve every poor result.

Importance evaluation is a fixed label-independent subset of at most1,500test date-hour keys ordered by SHA256(key). For each variant and each of the10predictor groups, replace its raw values using the same five prespecified permutations. Permutations operate within month×hour×workingday strata to retain those calendar contexts; strata of one remain unchanged and their frequency is reported. Joint-temperature perturbation permutes temp+atemp together with the same indices. Common indices across models/refits isolate training-history variation from new permutation randomness. Seed=20261028+repeat_index; retain stratum definitions, ordered evaluation keys and every permutation index/digest. Never fit a transformer on the permuted/test data.

Permutation importance is the increase in evaluation MSE over that model's unpermuted evaluation MSE, in count², averaged across five permutations. Retain all raw/permuted prediction exports and squared errors, including negative importance. This is a **model-reliance diagnostic**, not a plausible intervention or conditional causal estimator: individual temperature shuffles can break its relationship with feels-like temperature and other weather variables. The joint permutation preserves the pair's own observed relationship but can still break relationships with other covariates. Do not conceal that limitation or compare raw summed individual importance as if it were algebraically identical to joint importance.

Within each family, compare the12resampled rankings with the full-fit reference: top3 Jaccard, Spearman rank correlation with fixed alphabetical tie handling, top-ranked weather-group frequency, temperature-versus-atemp order reversals and the joint-temperature rank against the other weather groups. Weather groups are weather,temp,atemp,humidity,windspeed; for joint rankings replace temp and atemp with their joint group. Report all52variant values, not only a stability winner. Repeat variability is descriptive; do not pool resampled fits or date-hours into an unjustified significance test. No unsupported claim that additive or shallow models are necessarily stable.
